How it works

  • Paste text or upload PDF, DOCX or TXT, parsed locally with pdf.js and mammoth.
  • Keyword extraction from the job description using frequency plus a skills dictionary.
  • Parseability checks for sections, contact details, dates, quantified bullets and length.
  • A score breakdown by category with every rule visible, plus a Markdown report.
